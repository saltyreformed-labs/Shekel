{# The card's payment (plan step credit_card:CC-6, task plan#2679; rulings plan#2888 and plan#2889): the one payment every card is made with, and the one door that switches it. Below the Card terms card on the cash detail page (developer ruling R-CC25: the page that hosts every card form until CC-11's cockpit). Every card is made with payoff rows: each payday, a payment from the owner's primary account covering everything on the card. The owner opts into a payment scheduled with the card company only when they really set one up there, and it takes the payoff rows' place: the statement balance, the minimum, or everything on the card, on each statement's due date. The form is one radio group, so a submit names one of the four (app.services.card_payoff.PAYMENT_CHOICES' tokens), and the door (card.save_payment) switches the payment to it, asking first about any payment the owner typed over on a date the switch takes away. A scheduled payment falls on the statements' due dates, which the terms decide, so while the card is dormant its three choices are shown but not offered, with the reason; the door refuses them the same way. An archived card's payment is archived with it (task plan#3697), so the form is not drawn; the door refuses it too. Any other money sent into the card is an extra payment, which this payment's worked-out amount subtracts (ruling R-CC22), and the card says so. Context: account, card_terms (None while dormant), card_payment (routes/card/_page.PaymentOnPage, or None for a card with no payment, which no door makes). #}
