{# The card's terms form (plan step credit_card:CC-2, developer ruling R-CC24: one door, one schema), in a partial of its own since plan#2331's review (plan#2576) so the Card terms card and the version-correcting fragment (card.edit_terms) render ONE form. Every control renders every time, so a submit is a WHOLE version, stated from the "Effective from" date; the door it posts to (card.save_terms) creates that version or corrects the one already on that date (developer rulings plan#2333 and plan#2587). So the version the controls start from decides every term the owner does not retype, and this partial names it: * On the page (editing false) the controls start from the version in force today, and the date is left blank, as the APR's is: a change of terms must name its day, and a pre-filled date would turn a forgotten edit into a correction that re-cuts the past. * From a version's Edit button (editing true) they start from THAT version, its date included: the owner asked to correct it, and a correction from today's terms would write them over its other terms. The five NOT NULL terms are required controls (cash back pre-filled 0.00 for a card with no terms), the two nullable ones may be left blank. Rates are entered as percents and stored as fractions (E-28): the schema divides, the template multiplies back through |to_percent. Context: account, form_terms (the CreditCardParams version the controls start from, or None for a dormant card), editing (true when the owner chose form_terms to correct). The form itself is #card-terms-form, the element each Edit button's fragment replaces. #}
