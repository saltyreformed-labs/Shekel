Companion Accounts

Companion accounts can log in and manage entries on companion-visible transactions for the current pay period. They cannot see the budget grid, dashboards, account balances, or any other configuration. Mark a template as Companion Visible on its edit page to expose its transactions to companions. Each companion sets up two-factor authentication at their first sign-in.
